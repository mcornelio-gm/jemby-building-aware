Standard OEM electrical equipment, archetype attributes, cut-sheet discovery, and field collection bridge.

🗺️

The 5-Step Catalog-to-Twin Lifecycle

The Master Catalog is the single source of truth for standard equipment archetypes, bridging verified manufacturer data directly into rapid mobile field surveys and automated Single-Line compilation.

1 Domain & Archetype Discovery

In the Left Column, select the electrical domain (e.g. Power Distribution, Transformers, Branch Circuits), narrow by equipment type (PNL, SWG, XFMR), or click a brand (Eaton, Square D, Siemens).

2 Add or Clone Parts

Use [+ Add Part] to register a new SKU from scratch, or click [ 📋 Clone ] on any existing row to instantly spawn ampacity variants (e.g. 15A, 20A, 30A, 50A, 100A).

3 Define Dynamic Specs & Ratings

In the Part Editor Drawer, set standard ratings (Volts, Amps, kVA, AIC, Slots) and attach custom key-value specs (e.g. trip_curve: LI, enclosure: NEMA 3R).

4 Verify Cut-Sheets & Portals

Click [ 📄 View ↗ ] in the table to verify official PDF submittals or OEM portals. The search engine automatically quotes part numbers to prevent Google minus-sign bugs.

5 Automatic Field Unpacking in Survey

When an electrician in the Survey Workbench searches and picks this catalog model, JAMES automatically unpacks all electrical ratings, dimensions, and custom tags into the survey form with zero manual typing required.

Cascading Hierarchy Filters

🏷️

Dynamic Custom / Archetype Specifications

Every OEM part can store arbitrary key-value metadata that flows into data collection nameplate tags.

How Dynamic Specs Work:
  • Open the edit drawer on any part and navigate to Custom / Archetype Specifications.
  • Click [+ Add Attribute] to define keys like frame_rating, interrupting_rating_240v, or mounting_style.
  • When selected during field walk-throughs, the Survey workbench reads these specs and automatically populates the Custom Nameplate Tags list.
📄

Intelligent Cut-Sheet Search & Discoverability

Guaranteed zero link rot with dual-mode verified portals and high-precision quoted PDF queries.

Verified OEM Portals

Explicit cut-sheet links stored in docs.cut_sheet (e.g. Eaton PD series, Leviton product pages) open directly to the manufacturer catalog page.

Quoted PDF Query Engine

For parts without static URLs, the system dynamically launches: {Mfg} "{PN}" cut sheet pdf. Quoting eliminates Google hyphen word-negation bugs.
