JAMES Field Collector Workflow & Field Guide Digital Twin Guide

Step-by-step instructions for physical field surveys, panel schedules, photo notes, and hierarchy mapping.

🗺️

The 6-Step Field Data Collection Process

The Workbench is optimized for rapid walk-through electrical surveys on mobile tablets and iPads, seamlessly converting physical nameplates and breaker panels into a connected Digital Twin.

1 Select Domain & Equipment Type

In Column 1, tap the domain (e.g. Transformers, Switches, Panels, Sources). In Column 2, choose the archetype (e.g. LP, MDP, MCC). JAMES auto-increments tag numbers (e.g. LP-1 → LP-2).

2 Fill Nameplate & Location

In Column 3, specify the unique tag, voltage, amperes, AIC, and Room. A real-time ⚠️ Tag in use guard prevents duplicate tags in the facility stack.

3 Link Upstream Source ("Fed From")

Select the upstream parent in Fed From. Dropdown options automatically filter by electrical hierarchy (primary sources have no upstream, and terminal loads never feed upstream).

4 Populate Embedded Breaker Schedule

For panels or MCCs, scroll down in Column 3 to the live slot matrix. Click any slot to configure 1P/2P/3P breakers, trip ratings, and link downstream loads (excluding upstream ancestors).

5 Attach Observations & Camera Photos

Type field observations and use 📷 Snap Photo (iPad camera) or ➕ Add Photos to attach nameplate & condition photos directly to the record.

6 Save to Facility Stack & View SLD

Click Save to append the asset to Column 4 (Facility Stack). Then click ⚡ View SLD Diagram in the header to view the compiled Single-Line schematic.

⚡ Physical Bus Stab & Phase Matching (NEMA / UL 67 Standard)

In standard North American panelboards, bus stabs run vertically down the center. Odd-numbered slots are on the left, even-numbered slots are on the right, and rows alternate phases:

🔴 Row 1 (Slots 1, 2) → Phase A
🔵 Row 2 (Slots 3, 4) → Phase B
🟢 Row 3 (Slots 5, 6) → Phase C

The sequence repeats continuously (Row 4 = A, Row 5 = B, Row 6 = C) down the panel.

Circuit Breaker Inspector Features

1. Multi-Pole Ganging (1P / 2P / 3P)
  • 1-Pole (120V / 277V L-N): Occupies 1 slot on a single phase (e.g. Slot 1 = Phase A).
  • 2-Pole (208V / 480V L-L): Spans 2 consecutive slots on the same side, connecting across two alternating phases (e.g. Slots 1 & 3 = Phase A-B).
  • 3-Pole (208V / 480V 3Ø): Spans 3 consecutive vertical slots on the same side, connecting across all three phases (e.g. Slots 1, 3, 5 = Phase A-B-C).
2. Breaker Trip Ratings & Archetypes

Select standard trip ratings (15A, 20A, 30A, 40A, 50A, 60A, 70A, 80A, 100A, 125A, 150A, 175A, 200A, 225A). Choose from MCCB (Thermal-Magnetic), AFCI (Arc Fault), GFCI (Ground Fault), DF (Dual Function), MCP (Motor Circuit Protector), or LSI (Electronic Trip Unit).

3. Downstream Equipment Linking

In the breaker inspector modal, specify the Fed Equipment Tag (e.g. CH-1 or PUMP-1). This associates the branch breaker directly with downstream equipment in the single-line diagram.

4. Making Spares & Spaces

To clear a populated breaker and turn the slot back into an unassigned spare space, open the slot inspector and click 🗑️ Remove / Make Space.

📷 Field Photo Documentation & Portable Storage

All field notes and photos are stored directly in SQLite (model.db) and the companion model.jsonl as self-contained Base64 Data URLs. No external image servers or complex file syncing required.

📸
Snap Photo (Camera Direct)

Tapping 📷 Snap Photo triggers the native rear camera hardware on iPads, iPhones, and Android tablets, letting you snap nameplates, damage, and conductor conduit entries with zero extra taps.

🖼️
Add Multiple Photos from Gallery

Tapping ➕ Add Photos opens the device photo library to select multiple existing photos at once.

🔍
Fullscreen Lightbox & Quick Deletion

Click any thumbnail to open the fullscreen Photo Lightbox for inspection and one-click downloading. Use the red ✕ corner button on thumbnails to delete unwanted photos.

🛡️ Unsaved Changes Protection Guard

JAMES automatically tracks form modifications. If you modify nameplate specs, breaker schedules, or attach photos and click another equipment domain or stack asset without saving, an safety modal gives you 3 options:

💾 Save & Switch 🗑️ Discard & Switch Keep Editing
Facility Stack Grouping Views (Column 4)
  • 📍 By Room: Groups assets by physical rooms/closets — ideal for walking surveys room-by-room.
  • ⚡ Power Tree: Visualizes upstream-to-downstream distribution hierarchy.
  • 📦 By Type: Organizes equipment by class (Transformers, Panels, Switches, Loads).
Re-Editing, Duplicating & Deleting

Click any equipment card in Column 4 to load it into Column 3 with an EDITING: TAG badge. Use 📋 Duplicate to clone repetitive assets, or 🗑️ Delete with safety confirmation.

⚡ Real-Time SLD Single-Line Diagram Engine

JAMES compiles your physical survey model directly into an engineering Single-Line Diagram using the embedded Graphviz renderer.

⚡ View SLD Diagram

Click the top right button to open the interactive Graphviz diagram showing main distribution nodes, transformers, ATS switches, and branch circuits.

📋 Export JSONL File

Export your facility twin as a clean, standardized JSON Lines file (model.jsonl) ready for Git versioning, CAD integration, and analytical processing.
