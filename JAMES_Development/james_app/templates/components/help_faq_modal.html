⌨️ General Hotkeys

  • N Add Equipment Archetype
  • Cmd/Ctrl + O Facility Database Finder
  • ? Toggle Field Guide & FAQ
  • Esc Close Drawer / Modals

⚡ Diagram Controls

  • F Reset Fit to Viewport
  • + / - Zoom In / Out
  • H Toggle Flow (Top-Down / Left-Right)
  • M Toggle Macro / Detailed Mode
  • R Reload / Recompile Diagram

📱 iPad & Tablet Touch Gestures

  • Row Tap (Survey) : Tap anywhere on a survey row to instantly slide open the edit drawer.
  • Box Tap (Diagram) : Tap any cluster box or equipment node to open inspection drawer.
  • Breaker Tap : Tap any breaker row in the panel schedule to open the multi-pole inspector.
  • Pinch & Pan : Pinch on screen to zoom and drag canvas with one finger.

Panel Schedule Management

JAMES supports 1-Pole, 2-Pole, and 3-Pole branch and feeder breakers with odd/even slot interleaving (e.g. Poles 1, 3, 5 vs 2, 4, 6). Connected loads and AIC short-circuit ratings are automatically reconciled during single-line generation.
